Video files are processed to create and manage short-form clips. Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to use and publish the content.

Content and information may be transmitted to social media platforms when a user authorizes publishing. We do not sell personal information.

Users should protect their computer, application files, credentials, and connected account access. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.
